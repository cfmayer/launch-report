The Week in Launch

One launch since Thursday; H3 sends MMX toward Mars this afternoon

The only orbital launch anywhere since Thursday's issue was the classified USSF-481 mission, which a Falcon 9 lifted from Vandenberg at 8:01 p.m. EDT Thursday. That brings 2026 to 243 orbital launch attempts, 236 of them successful, according to GCAT. Bandwagon-5, set for this morning, slipped to Tuesday because of weather in the booster recovery zone, SpaceX posted.

Japan's H3 is due to launch the Martian Moons eXploration (MMX) probe at 3:41:03 p.m. EDT today, and the Japan Aerospace Exploration Agency (JAXA) has extended its reserve period to November 9. Eight more orbital launches are dated through Saturday: four Chinese, three by SpaceX and one by Rocket Lab. Rocket Lab pushed the first Neutron flight to 2027.

I · Recent Launches & Returns

USSF-481 heads south from California

The classified Space Force mission was the 243rd orbital launch attempt of the year and the 119th by SpaceX.

Falcon 9: USSF-481

Falcon 9 launched the USSF-481 mission for the U.S. Space Force from Space Launch Complex 4E at Vandenberg Space Force Base, California, at 8:01 p.m. EDT Thursday (00:01:05 UTC Friday), 17 minutes into its window, and the booster landed on its 28th flight, Spaceflight Now reported. The payload is classified. The rocket flew south, toward a polar orbit, according to Launch Library 2; GCAT logs the mission as TH-2.

Nothing else flew

No other orbital launch took place worldwide between Thursday's issue and this morning, according to Launch Library 2. Bandwagon-5, scheduled for 6:30 a.m. EDT today, was called off before Monday's countdown. "Now targeting no earlier than Tuesday, October 20 for Falcon 9's launch of the Bandwagon-5 rideshare mission from Florida due to unfavorable recovery weather conditions," SpaceX posted at 3:27 a.m. EDT.

Dragon at the station

The CRS-35 cargo Dragon, launched October 13, docked to the International Space Station (ISS) on Thursday morning and is to stay about a month (NASA).

New on the schedule. Galactic Energy's Ceres-1S sea launch and three Long March flights, an 8A from Hainan, a 6A from Taiyuan and a 4B from Jiuquan, have been added since Thursday (Launch Library 2). None of the four payloads has been announced.

Bandwagon-5. The flight also carries the second demonstration of Starfall, a SpaceX reentry capsule meant to bring experiments and in-space manufactured products back to Earth. The capsule is about 0.75 meters tall and 3.1 meters wide and can carry 1,000 kilograms, according to Launch Library 2.

III · Trajectories & Hazard Areas

H3 is on the pad for MMX

The rocket rolled out Monday afternoon in Japan; its spent stages are to fall in three zones off Japan's Pacific coast.

Launch day

The H3 carrying MMX left its assembly building for the pad at about 1:30 p.m. Japan time Monday, 12:30 a.m. EDT, the Yomiuri Shimbun reported. It is the first H3 to launch a planetary probe and flies in the H3-24 configuration, with two liquid-fueled engines and four solid boosters, the most powerful version. H3 Flight 8 failed last December; flights in June and August succeeded, the paper noted. JAXA's live broadcast begins at about 2:46 p.m. EDT (JAXA).

H3 Flight 10 timeline

According to the launch plan, the rocket turns to an azimuth of 90 degrees, due east, shortly after liftoff. The upper stage fires twice and releases MMX into a Mars transfer orbit 71 minutes after liftoff. V · Industry & Regulation

Rocket Lab's Neutron slips to 2027

Air pockets in a carbon-composite tank dome cost the medium-lift rocket its 2026 debut.

Neutron

Rocket Lab said Wednesday that Neutron will not fly until 2027. Inspections found voids, or air pockets, between carbon-fiber layers of a newly cured forward propellant dome for the first stage, Ars Technica reported. "They don't necessarily mean that the dome would fail in flight, but our practice is to proceed only when we believe the system has the appropriate margins," Rocket Lab founder Peter Beck said in a company video.

Engineers traced the voids to thicker composite sections added after a test tank ruptured in January. An aft dome made with a revised process has passed inspection. "We've solved the problem," Beck said. Qualification of the first stage and of the nine-engine Archimedes booster is still pending, and the rocket will then begin its launch campaign at Wallops Island, Virginia. Neutron was announced in 2021 with a first flight aimed for 2024 (Ars Technica; NASASpaceflight).

Starship plans

SpaceX removed the engines from Ship 42 for an extra cryogenic test, and Booster 22 awaits a static fire at Starbase's Pad 2 ahead of Flight 15, NET November 8, NASASpaceflight reported. Flight 16 in December would likely use Booster 23 and Ship 43. Starship has launched five times in 2026.

Comment deadlines

New Glenn at SLC-36. A virtual public meeting on raising New Glenn's cap from 12 to 50 launches a year is Thursday from 6 to 8 p.m. EDT; comments close October 30 (notice, docket FAA-2026-9841).

Part 450. Comments on five proposed changes to the commercial launch and reentry licensing rule are due November 4 (docket FAA-2026-9935).

Starbase. Scoping comments on up to 456 Starship launches a year at Starbase are due November 30, with meetings November 17 to 19 (Federal Register). No new FAA launch notices have appeared since Thursday (Federal Register).

Quick Hit

Starshield and Ukraine. Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said Sunday that he believed the U.S. government was behind the speed limits on Starshield-linked vehicles and drones; Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent said no one in the Trump administration had ordered them, CNBC reported.

Jonathan McDowell's General Catalog of Artificial Space Objects (GCAT) records 243 orbital launch attempts through USSF-481 on October 15, 236 of them successful.
